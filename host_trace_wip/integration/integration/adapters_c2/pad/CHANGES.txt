INTEG_PAD: the engine's padding, emulated in our SGLang arms for an A/B. Scratch copies only; the sources are unchanged.
Use them with ARMF_ADAPTER_DIR=land/scratch/integration/adapters_c2/pad. INTEG_PAD=0 is the default and means no padding.
In that case each copy binds self.forward = self._forward_unpadded at __init__, so model.forward is the source
adapter's own forward and the trace keys are unchanged. INTEG_PAD=1 pads. INTEG_PAD_DECODE_MAX_BS (default 512) sets the
decode bucket list's max.

Files (copied from):
- adapter.py: armF/adapter.py (arm F)
- adapter_cpp.py: armF/adapter_cpp.py (arm Fc)
- adapter_prep.py: armF/prep/adapter_prep.py (arm P)
- adapter_prep_cpp.py: adapters_c2/adapter_prep_cpp.py (arm Pc; it already has the PREP_ABC removal, adapters_c2/CHANGES.txt 1)
- integ_pad.py: new

1. integ_pad.py (new, ~140 LOC): Pad and check.
   - Pad.batch pads a decode's bs, or an extend's / mixed's token count, to SGLang's bucket and returns the padded
     ForwardBatch copy plus input_ids and positions. It uses one eager F.pad per padded tensor, as the runners'
     fill_from copies before replay.
     - Decode buckets: generate_decode_cuda_graph_batch_sizes(get_server_args(), 512). That is the serving list on a
       GPU over 160 GB (52 buckets). one_batch's resolved list ends with an exact bucket at max(--batch-size), so it is
       not used.
     - Prefill buckets: cuda_graph_config.prefill.bs (up to 16384), with can_run_graph's 2x padding-factor cap.
     - Fill values follow build_decode_registry: seq_lens, orig_seq_lens and seq_lens_cpu get the backend's
       get_cuda_graph_seq_len_fill_value() (trtllm_mha: 1); req_pool_indices, positions, out_cache_loc and input_ids
       get 0. seq_lens_sum grows by extra * fill and batch_size is the bucket.
     - A bs or token count past the list (or past the 2x factor) runs unpadded, as the engine runs it eagerly.
   - Pad.slice_out cuts the output's tensor fields to the raw rows (views, like the runner's [:raw_num_token]).
   - Pad.decode_size(bs) gives PrepF's padded bs.
   - Pad.stats counts per kind (decode / extend / prep): calls, padded calls, rows asked, rows computed and pad launches.
     unpadded_fields lists CUDA fields of the raw size that were left unpadded (none are expected).
   - check() compares a padded call's raw rows with the model's own eager forward on the unpadded batch ("bitwise")
     and on the padded batch ("bitwise_padded_eager": same rows and GEMM shapes).
2. adapter.py and adapter_prep.py, ArmF (~25 LOC each):
   - imports integ_pad and sets self.pad in __init__;
   - forward becomes the padding wrapper and the old forward is renamed _forward_unpadded. For decode the wrapper calls
     backend.init_forward_metadata(padded fb) again (_metadata's buffers at the bucket, or the backend's own init, as the
     runner's replay init), runs _forward_unpadded with self.check off, slices, and appends integ_pad.check when
     checking;
   - summary() gains "pad".
3. adapter_cpp.py (~30 LOC):
   - same init and summary changes;
   - new _pad_forward: the padded call goes through self.bound (ONECALL) or self.forward. install() points
     model.forward at it when INTEG_PAD=1, and the check setter leaves model.forward alone then.
4. adapter_prep.py and adapter_prep_cpp.py, PrepF (~20 LOC each):
   - the grab step runs through the padding wrapper, so the template's metadata is at the padded bs. _md_bs(bs) gives
     that bs for the template and regrow checks;
   - _prep_step's trace pads after the prep kernel. The padded bs is the metadata tensors' rows (passed in, so it is
     symbolic, with no bucket guard in the trace). It runs F.pad x6 in the graph, calls the forward at the padded bs,
     and slices its outputs to n. The batch state written back (input_ids, seq_lens, orig_seq_lens, out_cache_loc) stays
     unpadded;
   - decode() counts prep calls in Pad.stats;
   - adapter_prep_cpp.py also gains the forward wrapper and summary change (item 2).
