## Attribution Notes

- Grouped quant fusion, pointwise cat, and nested rows are primarily
  fusion/launch/materialization wins: old kernels disappear.
- The outer-sum and outer-softmax loop-ordering toggle rows are intentionally
  omitted from this representative-impact table. They are useful background
  sanity checks for the broad `loop_ordering_after_fusion` feature, but they are
  not credited as #179090 impact.
- `cvt_e8m0_rceil` is the representative same-kernel-count generated-body win:
  the faster operation is the SM100 PTX conversion, not fewer launches.
- Deferred assertions and deferred `copy_if_misaligned` are wrapper-level first
  launch wins. They should not be described as kernel-body speedups.
- The test-covered RMSNorm grouped FP8 quant paths are included as `2 -> 1`.
  The swizzled RMSNorm -> MXFP8 benchmark is the clean MXFP8 comparison and the
  one-kernel nested+inline-asm path is the reported result. The older
  non-swizzled scratch path that materialized `scale_e8m0.float()` remains
  investigation-only. The NVFP4 #183638 connection remains context-only in the
  local inventory.
